What the game does and does not do

This will not shorten anyone's waiting list or get a child the support they are owed. What it does is put a non-autistic player inside a system where depth is the strength and interruption is the cost, and make a game an autistic player can actually sit down with: no timer, no fail state, nothing missable, no sound unless you asked for it, and the whole world set to your terms before the first frame.

Something is about to ask for you.

Answering takes all of your depth. Holding it costs you something else.
